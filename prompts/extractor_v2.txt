{"vendor": str, "invoice_number": str, "invoice_date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "total": number, "currency": "USD|EUR|INR|GBP", "confidence": number between 0 and 1}

- vendor is the company that issued the invoice, not the recipient.
- total is the final amount due as a plain number (no symbols or thousands separators).
